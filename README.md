# Retail Sustainability Benchmarking Index
> **An Independent Comparative Climate Analysis of Tesco PLC, J Sainsbury plc, and Walmart Inc.**

##  1. Project Objective

The goal of this project is to deliver an independent, 1,500 word comparative sustainability report that evaluates **Tesco**, **Sainsbury's**, and **Walmart** against the exact same environmental criteria. 

The analysis looks directly at each retailer's **official annual sustainability reports** and cross references their claims with trusted, independent third party platforms (**SBTi**, **CDP**, and the **WBA**) to verify their actual climate progress.

### Core Deliverables
* **Tesco Performance Scorecard:** An objective evaluation highlighting where Tesco leads the market and identifying data backed areas for operational improvement.
* **Cross Border Normalisation:** A standardised data model adjusting for scale differences and isolating Walmart's grocery operations from its general merchandise footprints.
* **Target Accountability Assessment:** A verified side by side comparison checking public sustainability promises directly against independent, audited scores.

   

## Core Data Sourcing & Verification

To maintain complete analytical transparency, this study relies on verified figures extracted directly from the following independent frameworks:

* **SBTi (Science Based Targets initiative)**  
  Data is extracted from the master dashboard to benchmark verified 1.5°C and FLAG target timelines.
* **CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project)**  
 Official letter grades are analysed alongside detailed company questionnaires sourced from investor archives to isolate localised food and agricultural footprints.
* **WBA (World Benchmarking Alliance)**  
  Rankings from the Food and Agriculture Benchmark are utilised to grade retailers on food specific criteria like food waste and packaging.
* **Official Corporate Disclosures**  
  Baselines and qualitative strategies are pulled directly from each retailer's primary annual sustainability and ESG reports.

   

##  2. How the Comparison Works (Methodology)

To make sure a massive global company like Walmart can be fairly compared to UK focused grocers like Tesco and Sainsbury's, the data is adjusted in two ways:

1. **Carbon Efficiency (Emissions vs. Revenue):** Big companies naturally emit more carbon. To measure true efficiency, absolute emissions are divided by annual revenue to see exactly how much carbon each retailer emits per £1 Million in sales.
2. **Isolating Food Sourcing:** Tesco and Sainsbury's are primarily supermarkets, while Walmart sells everything from clothes to electronics. This study utilises **CDP Climate reports** to focus specifically on their food and agricultural supply chains.
